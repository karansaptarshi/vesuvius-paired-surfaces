Pilot surfaces

Community sheet coordinates come from ketchupbyheinz/vesuvius-scroll-survey, revision 1f12c1ceb14c1f24acc48249eaad60bcdf541721. The pilot footprints were resampled onto a 9.6 µm atlas. The source tags appear in each local-match.json. The archive adds local cross-scan calibration, coordinate conversion, held-out tests, and comparison panels.
https://github.com/ketchupbyheinz/vesuvius-scroll-survey/tree/1f12c1ceb14c1f24acc48249eaad60bcdf541721/results/meshes

TIFXYZ readers, writers and community data terms

The Python Vesuvius library implements the native TIFXYZ format. Ten surfaces were written and read back with the MIT-licensed implementation at this revision.
https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/tree/e0bbb8b40a2db58b1d71864f286eb85717e59e64/vesuvius/src/vesuvius/tifxyz
https://github.com/ScrollPrize/open-data

The community surface provider explicitly licenses the material in results/, including the source meshes used here, under CC BY-NC 4.0. The paired TIFXYZ data resample those meshes and transform them between acquisitions. The neighboring ridges also use local CT density.
https://github.com/ketchupbyheinz/vesuvius-scroll-survey/blob/1f12c1ceb14c1f24acc48249eaad60bcdf541721/LICENSE
